Junchao Lin 林筠超

I am a PhD student at the School of Electronic Information and Communications, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Robert Caiming Qiu, IEEE Fellow and Full Professor at the school.

My research focuses, in order, on efficient inference, implicit neural networks, and graph learning. I develop consistency distillation, learnable solvers, and structure-aware models for reliable and scalable intelligence at lower iterative cost.

Research

Across numerical solvers, implicit representations, and structured data, I ask one shared question: how can we preserve model capability and stability while making inference faster and modeling more faithful to data structure?

Efficient Inference

Reducing iterative inference cost through consistency distillation, solver-trajectory compression, and structure-aware updates, with controllable speed-accuracy trade-offs.

Implicit Neural Networks

Studying fixed-point modeling, well-posedness constraints, and reliable learnable solvers for deep equilibrium models and implicit graph neural networks.

Graph Learning

Exploring semantic graph learning, long-tailed graph learning, and large-scale graph representation through relations, structure, and message passing.
