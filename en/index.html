Research

Across numerical solvers, implicit representations, and structured data, the central question is: how can we preserve model capability and stability while making inference faster and modeling more faithful to data structure?

Efficient Inference and Optimization

Reducing iterative inference cost through consistency distillation, solver-trajectory compression, and structure-aware updates, with controllable speed-accuracy trade-offs.

Implicit Neural Networks

Studying fixed-point modeling, well-posedness constraints, and reliable learnable solvers for deep equilibrium models and implicit graph neural networks.

Graph Learning

Exploring semantic graph learning, long-tailed graph learning, and large-scale graph representation through relations, structure, and message passing.

  1. 2026ICML · Accepted

    Consistency Deep Equilibrium Models

    Junchao Lin, Zenan Ling, Jingwen Xu, Robert C. Qiu

    C-DEQ addresses the high inference latency caused by fixed-point iterations in deep equilibrium models. It interprets DEQ solving as evolution toward equilibrium along a fixed ODE trajectory, then uses consistency distillation to learn direct mappings from intermediate states to the fixed point. The model supports both fast few-step inference and additional iterations for a flexible cost-performance trade-off; under the same few-step budget, it substantially improves over conventional implicit DEQs on graph learning, image classification, and language modeling tasks.

  2. 2026IEEE TPAMI · Published

    IGNN-Solver: A Graph Neural Solver for Implicit Graph Neural Networks

    Junchao Lin, Zenan Ling, Zhanbo Feng, Jingwen Xu, Minxuan Liao, Feng Zhou, Tianqi Hou, Zhenyu Liao, Robert C. Qiu

    IGNN-Solver targets the slow, iteration-heavy inference of implicit graph neural networks and their difficulty scaling to large graphs. A learnable initializer first predicts a better starting point; a small graph neural network then parameterizes generalized Anderson acceleration, treating fixed-point updates as a graph-dependent temporal process and predicting subsequent states at low cost. Experiments retain comparable accuracy while delivering roughly 1.5–8× faster inference, with larger gains on larger graphs.

    DOIarXivCode

  3. 2025EAAI · 140:109647

    Semantic Graph Neural Network with Multi-measure Learning for Semi-supervised Classification

    Junchao Lin, Yuan Wan, Jingwen Xu, Xingchen Qi

    ML-SGNN addresses noisy, incomplete, or structurally insufficient input graphs. It constructs a semantic graph from structural context and node representations to capture global organization and complex semantic relations, then uses multi-measure attention to adaptively combine feature subgraphs built from different similarity metrics, reducing the bias of any single predefined metric. The model jointly exploits the original topology, semantic graph, and feature graphs; experiments and ablations on six real-world datasets validate each component.

    DOI

  4. 2023Applied Intelligence · 53

    Long-tailed Graph Neural Networks via Graph Structure Learning for Node Classification

    Junchao Lin, Yuan Wan, Jingwen Xu, Xingchen Qi

    LTSL-GNN addresses sparsely connected tail nodes and original graph structures affected by noise, missing edges, or perturbations. It jointly learns graph structure and tail-node representation enhancement parameters, using informative head nodes and multi-measure relations to optimize connectivity before strengthening tail-node features on the learned graph. Its iterative procedure lets structure learning and node classification reinforce each other; experiments on six real-world datasets outperform strong baselines, especially under graph perturbations.

    DOI

  5. 2021Journal of Computer Applications · 41(5)

    Adaptive Multi-measure Unsupervised Feature Selection Based on Graph Structure Optimization

    Junchao Lin, Yuan Wan

    SAM-SGO tackles two limitations in unsupervised feature selection: incompatible similarity metrics and nearest-neighbor graphs that may not form an ideal connected structure. It treats the similarity matrix as a learnable variable, adaptively combines multiple metrics, and imposes a rank constraint to optimize graph structure, while unifying graph dimensionality reduction, multi-measure learning, and sparse L2,0 projection in one objective. Across benchmark datasets, it improves clustering accuracy by about 3.6 percentage points on average over LLCFS, DGUFS, and SOGFS.
